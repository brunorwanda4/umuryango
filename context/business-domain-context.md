# Business Domain Context

Use this file when changing product behavior, domain models, workflows, permissions, or financial logic.

## Domain Model

- Family is the tenant. A user may belong to many families, but records and permissions are evaluated inside one family at a time.
- Core roles: Owner, Leader, Member. Leader powers must be permission-based, not unlimited by role name.
- Core modules: identity, family membership, permissions, finance, contributions, savings, debts, budgets, projects, announcements, events, documents, reports, notifications, audit.

## Financial Rules

- Family money records require `familyId`; personal money records are owned by the authenticated user and may use a hidden private workspace as their persistence boundary. All records require amount, currency, date, category, creator, and status.
- Do not store money as floating point.
- Keep approval status separate from payment status when both matter.
- Approved records are auditable. Rejected records keep reviewer and reason.
- Draft and rejected records are excluded from normal reports unless explicitly requested.
- Multi-currency is future scope, so currency must still be explicit now.
- Date filters must define timezone and inclusive/exclusive behavior.

## Workflow Rules

- Sensitive workflows must record actor, family, timestamp, target, status transition, and audit history.
- Use transactions for multi-step money changes.
- Attachments stay linked to the record they support.
- Notifications should be queued and duplicate-safe.
- Documents need explicit visibility, access checks, metadata, and version history where enabled.

## Domain Areas

- Income: money received by family or fund; configurable approval; optional receipts and source details.
- Expense: submitted spending or request; receipt policy; approve/reject/correction; rejection reason.
- Contributions: campaigns for monthly, event, emergency, or project money; expected vs paid; partial payments; overdue status.
- Savings: target amount, current amount, target date, contribution history, milestones.
- Debts: borrowed or lent direction, original terms, principal, repayment history, due dates, overdue status.
- Budgets: period/category limits, approved records compared to budget, auditable revisions.
- Projects: budget, timeline, members, documents, updates, linked expenses/contributions.
- Announcements/events: priority, pinned/urgent state, RSVP, attendance, reminders.
- Reports: generated from source records; include period, filters, generated date, generated by.

## Permissions To Consider

Family settings, member invite/approve, role assignment, income create/approve, expense create/approve/pay, contribution manage/record payment, savings manage, debt manage, budget manage, project manage, document upload/view private, report view/export, announcement/event manage.

## High-Risk Areas

Money precision, missing `familyId` checks, report totals, approval transitions, document access, audit completeness, duplicate payment/contribution submissions, notification fatigue.
